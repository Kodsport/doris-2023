\problemname{Songbook}
Doris will be the toastmaster at a dinner with her university chapter.
During the evening, they will of course be singing songs! 
As Doris is quite uninformed of the songs in their songbook, she has chosen to instead pick as many songs as possible during the $t$ minutes allocated for singing. 
To avoid boring the guests, each song can be sung at most once.
As she is otherwise busy with picking who will speak and what they should say, she has asked for your help in this endeavor.

\section*{Input}
The first line of input contains the integers $t$ and $n$ ($1 \leq t,n \leq 10^5$),
the number of minutes allocated for singing and the number of songs in the songbook.

The following line contains $n$ integers $s_1, s_2, \dots, s_n$ ($1 \leq s_i \leq t \cdot 60$),
where $s_i$ is the number of seconds she estimates that it will take to sing song $i$.

\section*{Output}
Print an integer: the minimal number of seconds it takes to sing the greatest amount of songs possible.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups.
To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.
